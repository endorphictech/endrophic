About Endorphic

A healthcare communication company, built by people who understand healthcare.

Endorphic is a 12-person team of writers, designers, developers and filmmakers — guided by a healthcare professional who has spent his career inside hospitals, not just around them.

Who We Are

We are a healthcare communication company.

Our purpose is to help healthcare organisations communicate with greater clarity, credibility and impact.

Healthcare communication extends beyond marketing. It influences patient confidence, professional reputation, organisational culture and public awareness. Every interaction — a doctor's introduction, a hospital website, a patient education video, an internal training programme — shapes perception and trust.

We help healthcare organisations communicate with purpose. Not as a general marketing agency that happens to take on healthcare clients, but as specialists who work exclusively within this field.

Leadership

Guided by clinical experience. Run like an agency.

Pushkar Deshpande
Founder & Director
  • Registered Occupational Therapist
  • MBA
  • Managerial roles across MNCs & hospitals
  • Technology enthusiast

Before founding Endorphic, Pushkar spent years working inside hospitals and healthcare organisations — first as a registered occupational therapist treating patients directly, then in managerial roles across MNCs and hospital systems after completing his MBA.

That combination — hands-on clinical practice, corporate management experience and a genuine enthusiasm for technology — is what shapes how Endorphic operates today. It's why every engagement begins with understanding the clinical and operational reality of a practice, not just its marketing needs.

"Healthcare doesn't lack good doctors. It lacks doctors who are understood. Endorphic exists to close that gap — with a team that speaks both languages: healthcare and communication."

Pushkar leads Endorphic's strategy and client relationships, working alongside a full in-house team to deliver every engagement — from brand identity to video production to ongoing digital communication.
