From Clinical Expertise to Patient Confidence

Challenge

30+ years of experience and 4,000+ surgeries weren't translating into an online presence that matched. Patients searching for a piles specialist had no easy way to understand his credentials or take the next step to book.

What We Changed

We built a credibility-first website structured around his specific specialty — backed by professional photography, clear experience markers, and a direct WhatsApp booking path.

What The Patient Now Sees

A confident, credible specialist who clearly treats their exact condition — with an obvious, one-tap next step to book an appointment.
